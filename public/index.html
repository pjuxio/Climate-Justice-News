How this feed works

What is this?

ClimateJustice.news is an editorially curated news feed focused on climate justice. Every article is hand-selected by an editor for its depth, relevance, and justice-centred framing — not just algorithmic aggregation.

Categories

Articles are categorised by topic. Use the chips at the top to filter:

  • Policy — legislation, government, regulation, COP summits
  • Community — grassroots, activism, Indigenous, protest, movement
  • Science — research, data, emissions, temperature reports
  • Environment — ecosystems, biodiversity, oceans, forests
  • General — everything else

Filters

Use Range to show only articles published within a time window, and Focus to filter by the region the article was assigned to during curation.

Editor Login

Enter your editor token to enable curation controls. Your token is stored for this session only.
